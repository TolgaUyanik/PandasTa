## Implementation summary (round 1 submission)

* `pandas_ta/statistics/ts_c3_20.py` — the indicator.
  `mean(r[t]·r[t-lag]·r[t-2·lag])` over the `length - 2·lag` triples inside a
  window of `length` simple returns, divided by the cube of those returns'
  sample standard deviation (`ddof=1`). Default `length=20, lag=1`, column
  `TS_C3_20_1`. A window whose returns vary only at rounding level
  (sd ≤ 4096 eps) is NaN rather than the ratio of two roundings.
* `docs/measure_ts_c3_20.py` — the run behind every number quoted anywhere:
  Gate A (hand-derived window, the imported and the retyped prototype, a
  `sliding_window_view` reimplementation of the issue's line, a plain-Python
  scalar loop, four priced variant readings), Gate B (five mutants ×
  three frames × 37 perturbation offsets), the noise floor and its
  crossover, Gates C/D, the synthetic distributions, the meaning section and
  every guard.
* `tests/test_ts_c3_20.py` — the test module.
* `docs/TsC320Measured.md` — the receipt.
* Wiring: `pandas_ta/statistics/__init__.py`, `Category["statistics"]` in
  `pandas_ta/__init__.py`, the `core.py` accessor,
  `tests/test_altport2_ports.py::CATEGORY_TOTAL`, `README.md`'s three counts
  and statistics list, `docs/MLCompanionContract.md`'s `SF` count, and
  `docs/IndicatorDictionary.md` regenerated by its generator.

Gate E was NOT run (session rule): nothing under `../Backtesting` was read
and no `docs/gate_e_ledger.csv` row was added.
