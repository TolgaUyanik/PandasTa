### Fixes applied, finding by finding

1. **MAJOR, the 1e+8 accumulator poisoning.** Measured properly before
   writing anything: the reach was taken on a **2,000**-bar frame (the
   400-bar one could not show whether it recovers) at four magnitudes, and
   the same frames were put through `ts_ac1_60` as a control.

   | planted | later bars emptied, of 1,799 | last bar that fires | max \|column − oracle\| after bar 200 | `ts_ac1_60` on the same frame |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1e+6 | 0 | 1999 | 7.859e-01 | 0 |
   | 1e+7 | 1,643 | 1859 | 3.868e+00 | 1,643 |
   | 1e+8 | 1,779 | 220 | — | 1,779 |
   | 1e+10 | 1,780 | 219 | — | 1,780 |

   So the threshold is nearer **1e+7** than 1e+8, at 1e+7 it half recovers,
   and the behaviour is pandas' `rolling.var` rather than this spelling.
   Written into the module docstring's NaN rules, the trust-test comment, the
   measured doc's NaN-rules table and a new Guards section, and pinned by
   `test_one_huge_return_empties_the_column_to_the_end_of_the_series` (four
   parametrizations, including the sibling control and that nothing before
   the planted bar moves).

   The reviewer's *alternative* fix — tighten the per-return bound to the
   surviving magnitude — was **declined**, with the reasons on the record in
   that section: the crossover is a function of the window's own scale rather
   than a universal constant, the only structurally local spelling costs 35 s
   against 60 ms on 400,000 bars, and bounding it here alone would make this
   port disagree with every neighbour that divides by a rolling variance.
   Taking the smaller-scope reading is this run's standing rule (manifest,
   Phase 2).

   the finding is written up above in full.

   docstring and the measured doc: the measured quantity is

   value.

4. **MINOR, the one-sided body-bar assertion.** Parametrized per frame and
