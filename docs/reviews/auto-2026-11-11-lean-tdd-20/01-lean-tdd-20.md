## Implementation

* `pandas_ta/statistics/lean_tdd_20.py` — the indicator.
  `r[t] = close[t] / close[t-1] - 1`, `squared = min(r - 0, 0) ** 2`, one
  rolling mean over `length` returns with `min_periods=length`, then
  `np.sqrt`. No floor under the root (measured, Gate A6). Closes that are
  NaN, infinite, zero, negative or subnormal are mapped to NaN first. Column
  `LEAN_TDD_{length}`, category `statistics`.
* `pandas_ta/statistics/__init__.py` — the export.
* `pandas_ta/__init__.py` — `Category["statistics"]`.
* `pandas_ta/core.py` — `df.ta.lean_tdd_20()`, forwarding `length` and
  `offset` under those names.
* `tests/test_lean_tdd_20.py` — 109 tests.
* `docs/measure_lean_tdd_20.py` — the harness that prints every number the
  receipt transcribes.
* `docs/LeanTdd20Measured.md` — the receipt.
* Counts: `README.md` headline and the statistics line,
  `docs/IndicatorDictionary.md` (regenerated by
  `python docs/gen_indicator_dictionary.py`),
  `docs/MLCompanionContract.md`, `CATEGORY_TOTAL` in
  `tests/test_altport2_ports.py`.

ASSUMPTIONS taken without asking (headless; see `00-manifest.md`): category
`statistics`; name `LEAN_TDD_20`; the target fixed at 0 rather than exposed
as an argument; simple returns; NaN warm-up; no ×100 factor; no Gate E
