# `ts-kurt-60` — task log

**Task:** port tsfresh's `kurtosis` of 60 returns as
`pandas_ta/statistics/ts_kurt_60.py` through Gates A–D, with the five wiring
touch points, a test module and `docs/TsKurt60Measured.md` (PandasTa issue
#93). Gate E and both repositories' suites are the launcher's.
**Lane:** main thread. **Status:** PASS not reached; four rounds run, the
round-4 findings applied without a fifth review (see *Open after round 4*).

## Implementation log

1. **Read the precedents before writing anything.** `git show --stat` of the
   two merged ports the brief names (#24 `pfe`, #26 `kalman_trend`) and, more
   usefully, the three most recent tsfresh ports on `dev`: `ts_binent_20`
   (#89), `ts_c3_20` (#88), `ts_peaks_20` (#92). Shape, docstring weight, the
   `NOISE_FLOOR` idiom on a dimensionless quantity and the measurement-script
   convention all come from there.
2. **First implementation: a hand-rolled numpy two-pass form** over the
   centred moments, with a per-column accumulation loop to keep memory O(n).
   It worked, and the first measurement run killed it: against the screen
   prototype it was bit-equal on only 38 of 1,440 bars (agreeing to 8.4e-15),
   so the port and the column issue #93's Gate E figures were taken on would
   not have been the same arithmetic. It was also 4× slower and 18× heavier
   than the alternative.
3. **Second implementation, shipped: keep `pandas.rolling(length).kurt()`**
   and own the NaN discipline around it. Bit-equal to the prototype on
   1,440 of 1,440 bars on each of three frames, and the numpy form became the
   independent oracle in `docs/measure_ts_kurt_60.py` instead of the
   implementation. ⚠ This was a decision taken because a run said so, not a
   preference; the run is §A2/§A4 of the receipt.
4. **Wiring**, five touch points plus four document surfaces: the module,
   `statistics/__init__.py`, `Category["statistics"]`, the `core.py`
   accessor, `tests/test_ts_kurt_60.py`, then `README.md`'s three counts,
   `docs/IndicatorDictionary.md` (regenerated by its own generator, not
   edited), `docs/MLCompanionContract.md`'s tag counts, and
   `tests/test_altport2_ports.py`'s pinned `CATEGORY_TOTAL` (325 → 326).
5. **`docs/measure_ts_kurt_60.py`** grew with each round: Gate A's five
   spellings and the exact `fractions.Fraction` oracle, Gate B's five mutants
   plus two that are reported separately, the noise floor, Gates C/D, the
   meaning measurements, the guards, the cost, the wiring.
