## What was built

**The column.** `pandas_ta/statistics/tf_dfa_100.py` → `TFDFA_100`: cumulate
the mean-centred last 100 simple returns into a profile, remove a
least-squares straight line from each non-overlapping box of 4, 5, 6, 8 and
10 bars, take the root mean square of every residual at each box size, and
fit a line through the five `(log s, log F(s))` points. The slope is the
column. Scale-free twice over — the price enters only through
`pct_change`, and a rescale of the RETURNS shifts every `log F(s)` by the
same constant, which cancels in the slope.

**The one deliberate departure from the screen prototype** is the spelling
of that last dot product: a weighted sum rather than `@`, because `@` on a
`(rows, 5)` block is not stable under the block's shape and this module
walks the windows in chunks. Measured both ways (§Z2, §Z, §A1).

**The two disclosures the ML consumer needs**, both measured: the iid centre
is 0.6289 and not the textbook 0.5 (DFA-1's finite-box bias on a ladder of
4..10 bars), and the column is unbounded — a frame with one 50% price gap
reads -0.2197 .. 2.3288.

`test_the_gate_e_row_is_owed_and_not_yet_recorded` asserts the ledger has no
