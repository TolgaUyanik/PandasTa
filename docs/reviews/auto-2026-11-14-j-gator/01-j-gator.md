## Implementation

**The column.** `pandas_ta/momentum/j_gator.py` ships one column,
`J_GATOR_13_8_5`:

```
mid     = (high + low) / 2
jaw     = SMMA(mid, 13) displaced 8 bars
teeth   = SMMA(mid, 8)  displaced 5 bars
lips    = SMMA(mid, 5)  displaced 3 bars
J_GATOR = (|jaw - teeth| - |teeth - lips|) / close
```

SMMA is read as Wilder's `alpha = 1/length` with `adjust=False`, seeded at the
first observation — the screen prototype's spelling. The three lengths and
their three lags are module constants (`JAW`, `TEETH`, `LIPS`), not arguments;
`MIN_BARS` = 21 is a floor on the input. The only guard is on the divisor: a
close that is not finite, not positive, or subnormal empties its own bar.
