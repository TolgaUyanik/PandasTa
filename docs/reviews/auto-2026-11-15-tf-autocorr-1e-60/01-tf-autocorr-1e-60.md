### The one design decision worth recording

The window's log prices are built by **cumulating the window's own log
returns** from zero, not by reading `np.log(close)`. The two differ by an
additive constant that the demeaning removes. The reason is Gate D: `8a / 8b`
is the same double as `a / b`, so an exact binary rescale cancels before any
transcendental runs and the column is bit-identical under x8 / x64 / 2\*\*-40
/ 2\*\*40 **by construction** rather than by luck. The cost — that it is a
divergence from the prototype's spelling — is measured in §A6 and comes out at
zero differing bars on four frames and at five price levels.
