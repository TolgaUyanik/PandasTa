## Summary

The mathematics and wiring hold up. The formula matches catch22's `FC_LocalSimple_mean_tauresrat` / `co_firstzero` (`while acf > 0`, `maxtau = size`, a mean-1 residual that is the first difference, z-scoring correctly argued to change no sign). All five touch points are present, with the accessor forwarding `length` and `offset` correctly. README, `CATEGORY_TOTAL`, the MLCompanionContract count line and the dictionary diff touch only this indicator, and the category line is alphabetical. The Gate B mutants have unique `old` strings, and that is asserted. Gate D covers ×8/×64/2^±40, the ×10/×3.7 tolerance with masks checked, and `0 < fires < n`. The "numerator is 1, so the column is 1/tau(returns)" degeneracy is disclosed loudly in the module, the tests and the receipt; whether to ship a 6-to-8-level reciprocal ladder under a "whiten ratio" name is the owner's call at Gate E. The cosine change to the bit-equal test is legitimate arbitration by an exact oracle, not a weakening. But this port repeats the failure CLAUDE.md names as the main historical defect: the receipt's headline Gate A dispatch claims (8,399 without AVX2/FMA3, window 1122 and its two values, the module matching the exact oracle on all 1,400 cosine windows under both dispatches) and the 12-frame 0.6304 figure come from runs the committed script cannot reproduce, even though both sibling ports already ship a `_cpu.py` reproducer for exactly this. Two files also give contradictory labels for the CPU run. Add the reproducer and the missing sections, or cut the numbers, and this passes.
