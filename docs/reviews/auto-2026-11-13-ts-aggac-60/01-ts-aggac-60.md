## Implementation, before round 1

1. Read `CLAUDE.md`, the issue text, `docs/issues/screen/ind_screen.py:630`
   (the prototype) and the two merged sibling ports on `dev`: `ts_ac1_60`
   (#94, `2d66dcf`) and `ts_cid_ce_20` (#95, `d30b226`).
2. Derived the port. tsfresh's
   `agg_autocorrelation(x, [{'f_agg': 'mean', 'maxlag': 10}])` reaches its
   numbers through `statsmodels.tsa.stattools.acf(x, adjusted=True)[1:]`,
   whose `acovf[k]/acovf[0]` is `sum / ((n-k) * population variance)` — the
   expression `feature_calculators.autocorrelation(x, lag=k)` spells
   directly. So the column is the mean over `k = 1..10` of `ts_ac1_60`'s
   statistic at lag `k`. That identity is the port's foundation, so it is
   measured (A2) rather than asserted.
3. Wrote `pandas_ta/statistics/ts_aggac_60.py`. The numerator uses
   `2uv = u**2 + v**2 - (u-v)**2` per lag, giving
   `sum u*v = length*var - head[k]/2 - tail[k]/2 - gap[k]/2`, where `head`
   and `tail` grow by one squared deviation per lag so the loop is `maxlag`
   passes and not `maxlag**2`.
4. Wrote `docs/measure_ts_aggac_60.py` (sections `a b cd meaning guards cost`;
   `cpu` added later) and ran every section.
5. Wired the five touch points plus `README.md` (329 / 337 / statistics 38),

   `tests/test_altport2_ports.py` (`CATEGORY_TOTAL` 329).
6. Wrote `tests/test_ts_aggac_60.py` and `docs/TsAggac60Measured.md`.
