# Task log — `c22-periodicity` (issue #126)

## Implementation, in the order it happened

1. **Read the ground rules before the mathematics.** `CLAUDE.md` (five wiring
   touch points, the Gate A–F table, "the dominant defect is a claim written
   before it was measured"), the issue
   (`docs/issues/125-c22-periodicity.md`), the owner's prototype
   (`docs/issues/screen/ind_screen_r3.py`: a rolling window of the last 100
   daily SIMPLE returns, NaN until a full finite window with `std > 0`,
   scored by pycatch22), and the nearest merged sibling
   `c22_ami_timescale` (#125) — same family, same window, same `ORD` shape —
   plus its receipt and test module as the template for this one.
2. **Derived the quantity from the definitions**, not from the prototype:
   Wang, Wirth and Wang (2007) via the hctsa operation `PD_PeriodicityWang`,
   which detrends with `spap2(2, 4, 1:N, y)` — a least-squares cubic spline
   on two pieces — takes `CO_AutoCorr(y, k, 'Fourier')` out to `ceil(N/3)`
   lags, and reports the first peak with (a) a trough before it, (b) a rise
   of at least `th` above it, (c) a non-negative correlation. catch22 fixes
   `th = 0.01`.
3. **Wrote the module** `pandas_ta/statistics/c22_periodicity.py`: windows of
   100 simple returns via `sliding_window_view`, chunked; the spline detrend
   spelled as a projection onto the QR factor of the truncated power basis
   `1, t, t², t³, (t−½)³₊` (the same function space, built once per `length`
   and reused by every bar); the biased autocorrelations; then a vectorised
   trough-then-peak scan over lags 2 .. 33.
4. **Wired it**: `statistics/__init__.py`, `Category["statistics"]`,
   `core.py` accessor, README's three counts and the statistics list,
   `CATEGORY_TOTAL` 360 → 361, `docs/MLCompanionContract.md`'s tag counts,
   and `docs/IndicatorDictionary.md` regenerated by its own generator.
5. **Wrote the harness** `docs/measure_c22_periodicity.py` (S, A–E, G, H, P,
   T, Z) and measured. The first draft carried TWO floors — an absolute one
   on the window's return sd and a RELATIVE one on the residual — and the
   relative one was found half-dead by measurement: on a frame whose returns
   are an exact cubic it blanked 1,306 of 1,400 bars and let **94** through,
   reading values (3 .. 9) a reader could not tell from a real frame's.
6. **Replaced both floors with one**: the same absolute 4096-eps floor
   applied to the DETRENDED root mean square. That blanks the cubic frame
   completely, is inert on all six realistic frames (0 bars differ from the
   unguarded spelling), and SUBSUMES the window-level rule — the spline's
   span contains the constant function, so the residual's sum of squares
   never exceeds the window's, measured on 9,800 rows with 0 exceptions.
   Simpler and strictly stronger than what it replaced.
7. **Section P (opt-in, GPL-3.0 black box) decided the one thing the
   definitions could not pin.** The reference agrees with the shipped
   reading on 2,794 of 2,802 windows (99.71%) and with the four other knot
   structures on 2,676 / 2,604 / 2,572 / 2,408 and with no detrend at all on
   2,330 — so `spap2(2, 4, ...)` was read correctly, measured rather than
   argued. ⚠ The same section also found what it could NOT settle: biased,
   adjusted and no-demean autocorrelations all score 2,794, so the ACF
   spelling was decided by the definition (hctsa's `'Fourier'` = biased) and
   the alternative priced at 0 to 5 bars in 467.
8. **Faced the 24 windows in 8,400 where this port and the reference
   differ** (Spearman +0.9980, worst single-window gap 24 lags). The harness
   records all 24, and for the 8 that fall in the stride-3 subsample it
   records which of the fourteen alternative readings matches the reference
   on each: every one of the 8 is matched by at least one, none by all, and
   the best single explainer (the knot at a third, 6 of 8) is itself not the
   reference's reading — it loses 124 windows where this one agrees. Six of
   the 8 hang on a quantity within 6e-03 of a decision boundary and two do
   not. What it is NOT is this module's conditioning, bounded at 2.5e-14 by
   §A1. Identifying the cause would mean reading GPL-3.0 code, which this
   port may not do. It is disclosed in the module docstring, the test module
   docstring and receipt §P, together with its consequence: the 2026-10-24
   screen's Gate E figure was measured on the reference's column and is an
   EXPECTATION for this one.
9. **Wrote the tests** `tests/test_c22_periodicity.py` (260 after round 1),
   pinning the
   detrend structure, the exact rational oracle, the cosine law, the
   sentinel's meaning and rarity, causality (sweep + six mutants), the
   bit-identities, the floor's cost including the frame where the sibling
   fires and this column blanks, bad data, and the owed Gate E row.
10. **Wrote the receipt** `docs/C22PeriodicityMeasured.md` and re-ran
    everything under `NPY_DISABLE_CPU_FEATURES="AVX512F AVX512_SKX AVX2
    FMA3"` (`docs/measure_c22_periodicity_cpu.py`): 260 passed there too,
    and §F1 tabulates the two groups of figures that moved, both rounding.
