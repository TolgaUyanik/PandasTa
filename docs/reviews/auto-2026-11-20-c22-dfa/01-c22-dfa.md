## The order the work actually happened in, because the first reading was WRONG

1. **Gate A from the definition, first attempt.** `SC_FluctAnal(y, 2, 'dfa', 50, 1, 2,
   1)` decoded as: z-score, decimate by the lag of 2, cumulative sum, root-mean-square
   linearly-detrended fluctuation over 50 log-spaced box sizes deduplicated to 21,
   then a two-piece straight-line fit of `log F` on `log tau` whose break position is
   reported as a share. The three things the definition does not fix were read as: the
   sum of the two residual NORMS, pieces sharing the break scale, and a floor of 6
   scales on each side.
2. **The black box said no.** That column takes NINE values where the reference takes
   six, and piles on the two extremes of its own range; agreement 1,826 of 8,400
   (21.7%). The reference's own histogram piles on 8/21 and 13/21 — the boundary
   signature of a NARROWER search.
3. **A grid, not a guess.** 2,304 candidate pipelines (§Q) pinned the data pipeline:
   every one of the top eleven rows decimates keeping the first point, takes the
   cumulative sum after, and builds the ladder from the decimated length. 560 more
   (§Q2) showed the fluctuation's divisor is nearly inert — dividing by `boxes`
   instead of `boxes * tau` shifts `log F` by `0.5 log tau`, a LINEAR function of the
   regressor, which changes no residual. 168 more (§Q3) closed it: squares, shared
   break, range 8 .. 13 — **294 of 294**.
4. **8 and 13 are two numbers, not a rule.** Feeding the reference other lengths gave
   it ladders of 16 to 47 rungs; its range is symmetric at every one, and
   `max(8, round(ntt/4))` fits all 32 where a flat 8 fits 18 and the three
   neighbouring roundings 28, 24 and 24 (§Q6). §Q7 then tested the rule by
   PREDICTION, window by window, across every ladder size: **3,084 of 3,084**.
5. **The whole thing re-measured.** §P1: bit-equal on **8,400 of 8,400** windows of
   six frames, 0 disagreements.
6. **Gate B found something structural**, not a defect: the decimation keeps every
   second return starting from the OLDEST, so the window's newest return — the one
   that needs `close[T]` — is dropped. Four mutants that look like look-aheads are
   therefore causal, and the gentle sweep has almost no power against the one that is
   not. Two instruments were added: a violent splice (20% daily vol, 3x jump), which
   takes the one-bar look-ahead from 5 of 35 offsets to 28 of 35, and a
   one-close-at-a-time dependency map, which answers the causality question directly
   (bar T moves only from closes in `[T-100, T-1]`, 0 at or after T).
7. **Two measurements corrected statements in the module's own comments**: the
   `isfinite(sd)` limb is REDUNDANT here (the zero-fluctuation guard blanks the same
   rows — §H2), which is the opposite of its role in `c22_ami2`; and the
   zero-fluctuation guard is REACHED on data with real variation (bar 754 of a
   zero-then-+1% frame, 17 of 21 scales with `F = 0` — §G3c), where the first comment
   said only a flat window could reach it.
